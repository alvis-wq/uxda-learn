Make your own copy of our design system template, and a folder for it on your computer.

**Name** always means your own name, for example **Alvis-DS-test-template**.

### What to do
1. On your computer, create the folder **Documents/UXDA-DS/Name-DS-test-template**.
2. In Figma, open the [design system template](https://www.figma.com/design/374PbreBmX1o0RkhwnJi1r/UXDA-Design-System-Template-V1?node-id=2368-82) and duplicate it.
3. Rename your copy **Name-DS-test-template**.

### What to send
The link to your copy.
