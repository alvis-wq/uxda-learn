Make your own folder in the UXDA Sandbox team in Figma.

**Name** always means your own name, for example **Alvis**.

### What to do
1. In Figma, open the **UXDA Sandbox** team and go to **Sandbox**. Click **Create**, then **Folder**.

   ![Step 1: the Sandbox page in Figma with the Create menu open and Folder highlighted](/challenges/sandbox-step-1.png)

2. Name the folder with your name, for example **Alvis**. Leave the other settings as they are and click **Continue** to finish.

   ![Step 2: the Create new folder window, with the folder name filled in and the Continue button](/challenges/sandbox-step-2.png)

3. Your new folder opens. It's empty for now.

   ![Step 3: the new, empty folder inside UXDA / UXDA SANDBOX / Sandbox](/challenges/sandbox-step-3.png)

The example uses a longer folder name — your name is enough.

### What to send
A screenshot of your new folder.
