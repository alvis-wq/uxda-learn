Check the health of the new **Name-DS-template** in your Sandbox folder: the library your screen uses. Not your earlier **Name-DS-test-template**.

**Name** always means your own name, for example **Alvis-DS-template**.

### What to do
1. On your computer, create the folder **Documents/UXDA-DS/Name-DS-template**.
2. Open that folder in a new Claude Code chat.
3. Run **/dsx-connect** with the link to the **Name-DS-template** in your Sandbox folder.
4. Run **/harvest**.
5. Open that **Name-DS-template** in Figma, run the **UXDA Design System Cheatsheet** plugin and open the **Health** tab.

### What to send
A screenshot of the health status.
