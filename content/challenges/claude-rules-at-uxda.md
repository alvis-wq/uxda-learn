## Claude rules at UXDA
The basics of our company Claude account: who to ask, and the few rules we follow.

**Our rules in short:**
- We're on the **Team plan**. It's fine for client briefs, notes and work files.
- **Never** paste passwords, API keys or access codes.
- **Remove real people's personal data** (names, account numbers, ID numbers) before uploading.
  Use made-up examples instead.
- If a client's contract says no AI or third-party tools, follow it. Ask your lead if you're unsure.
- Don't tell clients we use Claude for our internal work.
- Access or account problems: ask **Ernests**.

Read the rules, then confirm below.
