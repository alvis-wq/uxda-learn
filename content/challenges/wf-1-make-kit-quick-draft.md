## Quick draft with the Make Kit
**Time:** about 20 minutes

**The brief:** a banking app wants a **savings goal** screen. The user sees their goal (for
example "Summer trip — €1,200"), how much they've saved, and can add money.

### What to do
1. Open **Figma Make** with the **UXDA Make Kit**.
2. Describe the savings-goal screen and generate a draft.
3. Put the result into a Figma file.

### What to send
- The **link** to the frame in Figma. Check that anyone at UXDA can open it.
- **A short note:** what worked, and what you'd change.
